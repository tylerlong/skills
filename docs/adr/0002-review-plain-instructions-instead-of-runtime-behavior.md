# Review Plain instructions instead of runtime behavior

The `plain` source skill is an explicit exception to ADR 0001: revisions use a Skill contract review against a frozen Plain contract specification instead of runtime behavior scenarios, including smoke cases. This accepts the loss of runtime-compliance evidence because a model's failure to follow a complete, unambiguous contract is outside the skill's scope, while deterministic format and packaging checks remain. Plain selects and presents Available task evidence; the task or owning skill remains responsible for gathering evidence sufficient to support its conclusions. The specification is authoritative for its human owner and the review coordinator, while blind Reader profiles reconstruct the contract without seeing it; human-readable sections organize independently identified atomic obligations but carry no scoring meaning. Every independently losable meaning is a separate obligation with its modality, conditions, and exceptions, without targeting a smaller obligation count or mirroring Source wording. Contract Fidelity is bidirectional: every specified obligation must be recovered correctly, and no reader may recover a material extra or incompatible obligation or plausible materially incompatible alternative reading. Readers judge how faithfully, clearly, and readably the instructions communicate the approved contract, not whether they prefer its behavior. Instruction Clarity and Instruction Readability are relative to the best presentation achievable for the frozen contract and intended model reader: inherent semantic complexity, required precision, and intentional qualitative seams are not defects merely because they require effort. Every score below 4 requires exact instruction evidence, an avoidable defect, and a concrete valid improvement that can plausibly raise the dimension to at least 4 without changing approved behavior, losing any modality, condition, exception, protected boundary, or self-containment constraint, lowering the other dimension, or failing Contract Fidelity; 4 identifies the remaining minor valid improvement, while 5 marks the attainable frontier when no meaningful feasible non-trade-off improvement exists, not universal simplicity or perfection. Generic requests to shorten, simplify, or reduce cognitive load are not actionable. Mechanical Validity reuses the repository's existing `make validate` gate once per exact version; infrastructure failure stops without a verdict, and no Plain-specific validator is added. Each supported Reader profile reviews each exact version once: a completed incompatible interpretation fails without a diagnostic rerun, while unresolved contract intent stops for human decision. Contract-preserving revisions compare blinded Baseline and Candidate instructions under one specification; an approved contract change or specification correction qualifies only the Candidate under the newly frozen specification and promotes a passing Candidate to Baseline.
